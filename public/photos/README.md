# Destination imagery

All six WebP images in this directory are original AI-assisted **illustrative** scenes, not verified photographs of specific buildings or pools. The lighthouse and springs are scenic extracts of an original generated tourism design mockup. The Hundred Islands scene is a generated panorama. All were resized, cropped and converted to WebP to provide low-bandwidth local image assets. They do not reproduce Wikimedia Commons photo pixels.

For real tourism publishing, replace the files with authentic approved destination photographs of the same filename and use real photographer credits in `app/credits/page.tsx`.

The svg assets in `public/scenes/` are the initial hand-authored art and are retained for further customization.
